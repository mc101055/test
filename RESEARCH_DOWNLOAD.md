# Prediction Market Research Lab

[Download the complete research lab ZIP](https://github.com/mc101055/test/raw/refs/heads/research-lab-download/Prediction_Market_Research_Lab.zip)

This 7 MB archive contains the report, offline interactive dashboard, spreadsheet, executed notebook, historical market data, methodology, sources, supporting results, research code and tests.

After extracting the archive, open `Prediction_Market_Research_Lab/deliverables/Research_Report.pdf` or `Historical_Research_Dashboard.html`. The dashboard works offline without a trading account.

The study uses third-party reported historical Polymarket records and separately labeled synthetic experiments. Actual trader persistence remains unidentifiable because the acquired archive contains no participant histories. See the report for access, settlement-verification and execution limitations.
